About the Author

Pratap Titung

Writer. Thinker. Dissenter.

Pratap Titung is an independent writer and commentator committed to exploring the fault lines of contemporary society. With a background in political philosophy and journalism, Pratap writes at the intersection of power, culture, and human dignity.

Every article published here is a product of rigorous research, honest reflection, and a refusal to accept comfortable fictions. Whether it's dissecting electoral manipulation, interrogating media narratives, or celebrating grassroots resistance — this blog is where the work gets done.
