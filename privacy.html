Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Pratap Titung ("we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide:

  • Newsletter subscription: Your email address.
  • Contact form: Your name, email, and message.
  • Purchases: Email address and payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store your card details).
  • Donations: Amount and frequency preference; payment processed by Stripe.

We also collect minimal analytics data (page views, referral sources) through privacy-respecting tools, without tracking individual users across the web.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to your contact messages.
  • To process and confirm purchases and donations.
  • To improve the website based on aggregated usage patterns.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Period.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Stripe — for secure payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy governs how they handle payment data.
  • Email service provider — for newsletter delivery. Your email is not used for any other purpose.

5. Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies for basic functionality (e.g., remembering your newsletter subscription status). We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@prataptitung.com.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@prataptitung.com.
